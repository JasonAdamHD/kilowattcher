**docs-reserve-percentage** ✅: What does "reserve percentage" mean in my battery manual?

> The reserve percentage is the part of your battery's charge that it keeps back specifically for power outages. By default, it is set at 20%, which means about 2.7 kWh of your 13.5 kWh capacity is saved for backup. This energy won't be used during normal days to save you money but is reserved for when the grid goes down. You can adjust this reserve from 10% to 100% in the app. A higher reserve gives you more backup time but less energy to save money on daily costs, while a lower reserve gives more savings but less outage protection.

**docs-operating-modes** ✅: What operating modes does my battery have?

> Your VoltNest 13 battery has three operating modes. Self-Powered stores extra solar energy during the day to use in the evening and overnight.
