**docs-reserve-percentage** ✅: What does "reserve percentage" mean in my battery manual?

> The reserve percentage in your VoltNest 13 battery is the portion of the charge it keeps saved specifically for power outages. It won’t let the battery discharge below this level during normal days to make sure you have backup energy when the grid goes down. By default, this is set to 20%, which means about 2.7 kWh of the battery’s 13.5 kWh capacity is reserved for outages. You can adjust this in the app from 10% to 100%. A higher reserve means more backup power but less energy available to save money on daily use, and a lower reserve means more daily savings but less outage protection.
