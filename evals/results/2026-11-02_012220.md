**docs-reserve-percentage** ✅: What does "reserve percentage" mean in my battery manual?

> The reserve percentage is the portion of your VoltNest 13 battery's charge that it keeps back specifically for power outages. It won't discharge below this level during normal days to save energy for when the grid goes down. The default reserve is 20%, about 2.7 kWh out of your 13.5 kWh capacity. You can adjust the reserve from 10% to 100%, with a higher reserve giving more backup but less daily savings, and a lower reserve giving more daily savings but less backup.
