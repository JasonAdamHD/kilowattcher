**docs-reserve-percentage** ✅: What does "reserve percentage" mean in my battery manual?

> The reserve percentage is the portion of your VoltNest 13 battery’s charge that it keeps back specifically for power outages. It won’t discharge below this level during normal use to save energy for when the grid goes down. The default reserve is 20%, or about 2.7 kWh, but you can adjust it from 10% to 100% in 5% steps. A higher reserve means more backup power but less energy to use for saving money daily, while a lower reserve means more savings day to day but less outage protection.
