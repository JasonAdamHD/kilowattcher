**docs-reserve-percentage** ✅: What does "reserve percentage" mean in my battery manual?

> The reserve percentage in your VoltNest 13 battery is the share of the battery's charge that it keeps back specifically for power outages. It doesn't discharge below this level during normal days to save that energy for when the grid goes down. The default reserve is 20%, about 2.7 kWh of the 13.5 kWh capacity, but you can adjust it between 10% and 100%. A higher reserve means more backup during an outage but less energy for saving money day to day, while a lower reserve means more daily savings but less backup protection.
