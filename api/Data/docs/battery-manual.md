# VoltNest 13 Owner's Guide

## Overview

The VoltNest 13 is a wall-mounted home battery that stores energy from your rooftop solar or the grid and powers your home when you need it. It holds 13.5 kWh of usable energy and can charge or discharge at up to 5.0 kW continuously, which is enough to run most of a typical home at once. Each home has one VoltNest 13, installed next to the main electrical panel. The battery works on its own once it is set up: it decides when to charge and discharge based on the operating mode and reserve percentage you choose in the app.

## Reserve percentage

The reserve percentage is the share of the VoltNest 13's charge that it keeps back for power outages. During normal days, the battery will not discharge below this level to save you money; that energy waits for the moment the grid goes down. The default reserve is 20%, or about 2.7 kWh of the 13.5 kWh capacity. You can change it in the app from 10% to 100% in steps of 5%. A higher reserve gives you more backup during an outage but leaves less energy for avoiding peak prices. A lower reserve saves more money day to day but gives you less protection.

## Operating modes

The VoltNest 13 has three operating modes. Self-Powered stores extra solar during the day and uses it to power your home in the evening and overnight. Time-Based Control follows your electricity plan: it avoids buying power during peak hours and can charge from the grid when prices are lowest. Backup Only keeps the battery full and saves all of its energy for outages. You can switch modes at any time in the app. Grid charging is only allowed in Time-Based Control.

## Reading the status screen

The VoltNest 13 status screen shows three things. State of charge is the percentage of the 13.5 kWh capacity currently stored; 50% means about 6.75 kWh. Power is how fast energy is flowing right now, in kW: a battery discharging at 1.2 kW is supplying 1.2 kWh to your home every hour. The status label tells you whether the battery is charging, discharging, or idle. Power and stored energy are different: power is the rate, stored energy is the amount left.

## Charging from the grid

In Time-Based Control mode, the VoltNest 13 can charge from the grid during your plan's cheapest hours. It charges at up to 5.0 kW, so going from empty to full takes about 2.7 hours. Some energy is lost in every charge and discharge: round-trip efficiency is about 90%, so storing 1 kWh from the grid gives you about 0.9 kWh back later. Grid charging pays off when the price difference between cheap and peak hours is larger than that 10% loss, and when your solar forecast is too low to fill the battery on its own.

## Care and safety

The VoltNest 13 needs no routine maintenance. Keep the vents clear and do not stack items against the unit. Never open the battery case or the electrical panel it connects to. Installation, wiring changes, moving the battery, and repairs must be done by a licensed electrician. If the status screen shows a fault, or you notice heat, smoke, or a burning smell, turn the battery off in the app, keep people away from the unit, and call a licensed electrician or emergency services.
