# Helio PowerCell 10 User Manual

## Overview

The Helio PowerCell 10 from Helio Systems is a compact home battery that stores energy from your rooftop solar or the grid and powers your home when you need it. It holds 10 kWh of usable energy and can charge or discharge at up to 3.8 kW continuously, which covers lighting, the refrigerator, and most everyday appliances at once, but not several large loads together. The Helio PowerCell 10 mounts indoors or in a garage and is managed from the Helio app, where you choose its operating mode and reserve percentage.

## Reserve percentage

The reserve percentage is the share of the Helio PowerCell 10's charge kept back for power outages. On normal days the battery will not discharge below this level; that energy waits for the grid to go down. The default reserve is 15%, or 1.5 kWh of the 10 kWh capacity, and you can change it in the Helio app from 10% to 100% in steps of 5%. The Helio PowerCell 10 also keeps a fixed 10% backup floor it never goes below, even in an outage, to protect the cells. A higher reserve means more backup and less energy for avoiding peak prices.

## Operating modes

The Helio PowerCell 10 has three operating modes. Solar First stores extra solar during the day and uses it to power your home in the evening, keeping grid use as low as possible. Cost Saver follows your electricity plan: it avoids buying power during peak hours and can charge from the grid when prices are lowest. Storm Guard keeps the battery full and saves all of its energy for outages. You can switch modes at any time in the Helio app. Grid charging is only allowed in Cost Saver mode.

## Reading the status display

The Helio PowerCell 10 status display shows the state of charge as a percentage of its 10 kWh capacity, so 50% means 5 kWh stored. It also shows power flow in kW, with an arrow pointing in when the battery is charging and out when it is discharging, and a leaf icon when the battery is running on solar alone. Power is the rate energy is moving right now; stored energy is how much is left.

## Charging from the grid

In Cost Saver mode, the Helio PowerCell 10 can charge from the grid during your plan's cheapest hours. It charges at up to 3.8 kW, so going from the 10% floor to full takes about 2.4 hours. Round-trip efficiency is about 88%: storing 1 kWh from the grid gives you about 0.88 kWh back later. Grid charging pays off when the gap between cheap and peak prices is bigger than that 12% loss and your solar forecast is too low to fill the battery on its own.

## Care and safety

The Helio PowerCell 10 needs no routine maintenance, but keep at least 15 cm of clear space around its vents and keep it out of direct sun. Never open the battery case or the electrical panel it connects to. Installation, moving the battery, wiring changes, and repairs must be done by a licensed electrician. If the status display shows a red fault light, or you notice heat, smoke, or a burning smell, switch the battery off in the Helio app, keep people away, and call a licensed electrician or emergency services.
