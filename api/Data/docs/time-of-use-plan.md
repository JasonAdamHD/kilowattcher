# Brightline Energy Sunrise Saver Plan

## How time-of-use pricing works

The Sunrise Saver plan from Brightline Energy is a time-of-use plan: the price you pay for each kWh of electricity depends on the time of day you use it. Power is cheapest overnight, when demand on the grid is low, and most expensive in the late afternoon and evening, when everyone gets home and turns things on. You save money on the Sunrise Saver plan by shifting energy use away from peak hours, either by running appliances at cheaper times or by letting a home battery cover the expensive hours.

## Rate periods and prices

The Sunrise Saver plan has four rate periods each day. Super off-peak runs from 12 AM to 6 AM at $0.11 per kWh, the cheapest time to use or store power. Off-peak runs from 6 AM to 4 PM at $0.24 per kWh. Peak runs from 4 PM to 9 PM at $0.48 per kWh, the most expensive time of day. Off-peak returns from 9 PM to 12 AM at $0.24 per kWh. Peak power costs more than four times as much as super off-peak power, so moving even a few kWh out of the 4 PM to 9 PM window makes a noticeable difference.

## Weekends and holidays

On the Sunrise Saver plan, the same rate periods and prices apply every day of the year, including weekends and public holidays. There is no separate weekend schedule: 12 AM to 6 AM is always super off-peak at $0.11 per kWh, and 4 PM to 9 PM is always peak at $0.48 per kWh. This makes the plan easy to plan around, because the cheapest and most expensive hours never move.

## Solar export credit

When your rooftop solar produces more than your home and battery can use, the extra energy flows back to the grid. On the Sunrise Saver plan, Brightline Energy credits exported solar at $0.07 per kWh. That credit is lower than every purchase price on the plan, including the $0.11 super off-peak rate. Storing extra solar in your battery and using it during peak hours is usually worth far more than exporting it, because each stored kWh can replace power that would cost $0.48 per kWh.

## Fixed monthly charge

The Sunrise Saver plan includes a fixed service charge of $12.00 per month. Brightline Energy charges this amount whether you use a lot of electricity or very little, and it does not change with time-of-use periods. Everything else on your bill comes from the energy you buy at each period's price, minus any credit for solar you export.

## Tips to save on this plan

To save on the Sunrise Saver plan, run large appliances such as the dishwasher, washing machine, and dryer before 4 PM or after 9 PM. Charge an electric vehicle between 12 AM and 6 AM, when power costs $0.11 per kWh. Let your home battery cover your home's use during the 4 PM to 9 PM peak. Pre-cool your home in the early afternoon on hot days so the air conditioner works less during peak hours.
